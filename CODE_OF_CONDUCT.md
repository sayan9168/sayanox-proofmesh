# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, collaborative, and focused on improving the software.

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks are not acceptable.
- Assume good faith; disagree on ideas, not people.
- Security reports should follow SECURITY.md.

Maintainers may remove content or ban participants who violate these norms.

For issues that cannot be resolved in public, contact the maintainer listed in `pyproject.toml`.
